Pixallery

A private photo gallery for Android, with family backup.

Your photos, one timeline

See the photos on your phone and the ones you've backed up together, in one place.

Back up to your family drive

Copy photos and videos to a shared drive at home, so the whole family's memories are kept safe together.

Back up to Google Drive

Optionally back up to one or more of your own Google accounts. When one is full, Pixallery moves on to the next. Pixallery can only see the files it puts there itself.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no Pixallery servers. Face and place information is worked out on your phone and stays there. Private backups are locked so only your family can open them.
