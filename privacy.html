Privacy policy

Pixallery · Last updated 14 November 2026

Pixallery is a photo gallery app for Android. This page explains, in plain words, what it does with your information.

The short version

  • Pixallery has no servers of its own. We never receive your photos or any information about you.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your photos only go where you choose to back them up: your family drive, your own Google Drive, or both.

On your phone

Pixallery reads the photos and videos on your phone so it can show them. Things like faces, places, tags and memories are worked out on your phone and stay on your phone.

Family drive backup

If you set it up, Pixallery copies your photos and videos to a shared drive that you or your family own (for example, a drive on your home network). If you choose Private backup, the copies are encrypted so only members of your family can open them.

Google Drive backup

If you add a Google account, Pixallery asks Google for the smallest kind of access available: permission to see and change only the files Pixallery creates in your Google Drive (Google calls this drive.file). Pixallery cannot see, open or change any other files in your Google Drive, Gmail or Google Photos.

  • Pixallery uses this access only to save your backups into a folder called "Pixallery", to show them in your timeline, and to delete them when you ask.
  • Your Google data goes straight between your phone and Google. It never passes through anyone else.
  • Your Google data is not shared with anyone, sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models.
  • You can stop at any time: remove the account in Pixallery, or take away access in your Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services". Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Pixallery's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Maps

When you open the map of your photos, the map images are downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, they can see which part of the map is being loaded. Your photos are not sent.

Children

Pixallery is a family app but does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
